Privacy & Data Protection Principles

This extension was designed with privacy-by-default architecture. Your data belongs entirely to you.

  • Zero Telemetry & Analytics We do not collect, track, log, or transmit any analytics, browsing history, device fingerprints, or behavioral data to any third-party server.
  • 100% Local Device Storage Your tracked streamer list, polling interval preferences, sound toggles, and notification settings are stored locally on your device via Chrome's native chrome.storage.local API. No cloud databases or external sync servers are involved.
  • Direct API Inquiries Status checks are performed directly from your browser to public Kick channel endpoints strictly to determine live stream availability, viewer count, and category title.
  • Immediate Data Erasure When you delete a streamer from your dashboard, their record is instantly and permanently deleted from local memory. Uninstalling the extension completely removes all extension data from your machine.

Permissions Explained

  • Alarms (alarms) Used to wake up the background worker at your configured interval (e.g. every 2 minutes) to perform stream status checks.
  • Desktop Notifications (notifications) Used to pop up native system notifications when your tracked streamers transition from offline to live.
  • Storage (storage) Used to save your list of favorite creators and preferences on your local browser profile.
  • Host Access (https://kick.com/*) Required to fetch live status updates and channel metadata directly from Kick's public endpoints.
