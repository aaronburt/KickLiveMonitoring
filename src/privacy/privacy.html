Privacy & Zero-Data Collection Policy

Your privacy is guaranteed by design. The extension operates entirely client-side on your local browser.

🛡️ Zero Telemetry & Analytics
We do not collect, track, log, or transmit any analytics, browsing history, device fingerprints, or behavioral data to any third-party server.
💾 Local Storage & Optional Chrome Cloud Sync
Your tracked creators list and preferences stay on your device via chrome.storage.local. When signed in, your slug watchlist syncs strictly across your own Chrome accounts using Google's native chrome.storage.sync quota. No custom external database is used.
⚡ Direct Platform Requests
Status checks are performed directly from your browser to public Kick endpoints (e.g. kick.com/api/v2/channels/{username}) strictly to determine live broadcast status, category title, and viewer count.
🗑️ Instant Data Erasure
Removing a streamer immediately purges all their metadata from storage. Uninstalling the extension completely deletes all associated local data instantly.

Browser Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose
alarms Wakes up the background service worker on your chosen schedule (e.g. every 2 minutes) to check if tracked creators have gone live.
notifications Displays desktop notifications when a tracked creator transitions from offline to live.
storage Saves your tracked creator list, notification settings, sort order, and UI zoom preferences locally.
activeTab Auto-detects when you open the popup on a Kick channel page to prefill the creator's username for 1-click tracking.
https://kick.com/api/* Allows querying Kick's public channel status API endpoints directly from your browser without third-party proxy servers.
